TapConvert – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

The short version

TapConvert processes your photos, videos, audio and PDFs entirely on your device. Your media files are never uploaded to our servers — we operate no servers that receive your media. No accounts, no sign-in, no media tracking.

What stays on your device

All conversions (video compression, photo resizing, MP3 extraction, PDF tools) run locally with on-device libraries. Converted files are saved to your device's standard media folders (Pictures/TapConvert, Movies/TapConvert, Documents/TapConvert). A lifetime-saved counter is stored locally on your device.

Advertising (free version)

The free version of TapConvert shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID) and app-interaction data to serve and measure ads, per Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or opt out of ad personalization in your Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. TapConvert Pro removes all ads.

Purchases (Pro)

Pro subscriptions and lifetime purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not see or store your payment details; they are handled by Google under the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Permissions

TapConvert requests only the permissions it needs to read media you select and save results back to your device (photo/video storage access, notifications for finished conversions on newer Android versions). The app works fully offline; internet access is used only for ads in the free version.

Children

TapConvert is a general-utility app not directed at children under 13. No personal data is collected by us from any user.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the developer via the support email listed on our Google Play store listing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new date above.
